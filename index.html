How it works

Clear scope. No surprises.

  1. Scoping call

    We learn what's driving the request and recommend the right service, even if it's a smaller one.

  2. Proposal

    A written scope, timeline and quote. Rules of engagement agreed before any testing.

  3. Engagement

    We do the work and keep you updated. Critical findings get escalated right away.

  4. Report & readout

    Plain-English results for leadership, technical detail for your team, and a walkthrough call.

  5. Follow-through

    Retesting, remediation help and ongoing advisory, so findings actually get fixed.
