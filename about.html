About

Practitioners, not salespeople.

Topcu Industries is a cybersecurity firm built by certified practitioners who have worked both sides: breaking in and responding when someone else does. We bring that experience to companies that need serious security without a serious security budget.

What we believe

Security should make sense to the people paying for it.

01Think like an attacker

Every recommendation we make is grounded in how real attacks unfold, not checklists for their own sake.

02Defend like an operator

Fixes have to work in your environment, with your tools and your team. We design for the people who will maintain it.

03Plain language

If leadership can't understand the risk, they can't make good decisions about it. We explain things clearly.

04Confidential by default

Your findings, your data and the fact that you work with us stay private. NDAs are standard.
