Red Team

Adversary emulation that answers the question leadership actually cares about: if someone came after us, would we notice, and could we stop them?

A pentest asks “what's vulnerable?” A red team asks “can we get to what matters, and will anyone catch us?” We pick an objective with you, like your finance system, customer data or source code, and pursue it the way a real threat actor would.

When it's over, we replay the whole attack with your defenders so every gap becomes a detection you didn't have before.

Good fit if

  • You've done pentests and want to test detection and response, not just vulnerabilities
  • You've invested in security tools (EDR, SIEM, MDR) and want to know if they work
  • Leadership or the board wants a realistic answer to “could we be breached?”

What's included

Objective-based scenarios

Clear goals agreed up front, such as reaching payroll, exfiltrating test data or taking over a cloud tenant.

Threat-informed tradecraft

Techniques modeled on real groups that target companies like yours, mapped to MITRE ATT&CK.

Initial access options

Phishing, external exploitation, or an assumed-breach starting point, whichever fits your goals.

Purple team debrief

We replay the attack step by step with your defenders and tune detections together.

Questions

Are we ready for a red team?

If you don't yet have basics like endpoint protection, central logging and someone watching alerts, start with a penetration test or a blue team assessment. We'll tell you honestly which one gets you more value.

Who at our company knows it's happening?

A small “white cell” of people you choose, usually an executive sponsor and one technical contact. Everyone else experiences it like a real attack.

Is it safe?

Yes. Rules of engagement, off-limits systems and emergency stop procedures are agreed in writing before we start.
