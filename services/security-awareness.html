Security Awareness & Phishing

Most breaches start with a person, not a server. Train your team to spot phishing, wire fraud and social engineering, and to report it fast.

Generic annual training videos don't change behavior. Realistic practice does. We run phishing simulations modeled on the lures attackers are actually sending, and pair them with short, relevant training.

We measure success by reporting rate, not just click rate. A team that reports quickly is your best early-warning system.

Good fit if

  • Finance and executive teams targeted by invoice fraud and CEO impersonation
  • SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI and insurance requirements for annual training
  • A recent phishing incident or close call

What's included

Phishing simulations

Ongoing or one-time campaigns using realistic, current lures, tailored to your business.

Live training sessions

Engaging, practical sessions over video, built around real examples.

High-risk role training

Focused sessions for finance, HR, executives and IT admins on BEC, wire fraud and account takeover.

Reporting culture

Help setting up an easy “report phishing” process and rewarding people who use it.

Questions

Won't employees feel tricked?

Not if it's done right. We design no-shame programs: people who click get a short, helpful lesson, not a scolding, and people who report get recognized.

Phone and text-message testing can be added on request. Ask us about it on your scoping call.
