Penetration Testing

A hands-on test of your applications, networks and cloud by people who think like attackers, and a report your engineers can actually fix from and your customers will accept.

Automated scanners find the obvious. A penetration test finds what an attacker would actually use: chained weaknesses, broken business logic, misconfigured cloud permissions, and the one forgotten server nobody patched.

We verify every finding by hand, so you get no scanner noise and no false positives. Just what's real, how bad it is, and how to fix it.

Good fit if

  • SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA or PCI evidence you need for an audit
  • An enterprise customer asking for a recent third-party pentest
  • Cyber insurance applications and renewals
  • Before a major launch or after big infrastructure changes

What we test

External network

Everything your company exposes to the internet: hosts, VPNs, email, remote access and forgotten services.

Internal network & Active Directory

What an attacker can reach once they're inside, from a phished laptop to domain admin.

Web applications & APIs

Authentication, authorization, business logic, injection and data exposure, tested against OWASP and beyond.

Cloud environments

AWS, Azure, GCP and Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace configuration, identity and permissions review.

Questions

Will testing break production?

We agree on rules of engagement, testing windows and off-limits systems before we start, and we coordinate with your team throughout. Anything risky gets discussed with you first.

Is a pentest the same as a vulnerability scan?

No. A scan lists known issues automatically. A pentest is a person actively trying to break in: confirming what's exploitable, chaining weaknesses together and testing logic no scanner understands.

How long does it take?

It depends on scope. You'll get a clear timeline and fixed scope in your proposal before anything starts.
