Tabletop Exercises

Rehearse a ransomware attack, data breach or wire-fraud scam in a conference room, before it happens for real.

When an incident hits, the hardest problems usually aren't technical. Who decides to shut systems down? Who calls the insurer, the lawyer, the customers? Do you pay?

A tabletop exercise (TTX) walks your team through a realistic scenario, one development at a time, so those decisions get made calmly now instead of in a panic later. Our facilitators are certified incident handlers, so the scenarios feel like the real thing.

Good fit if

  • Leadership teams who have never practiced a major incident together
  • SOC 2, HIPAA and cyber insurance requirements to test your incident response plan
  • A new or updated IR plan you want to pressure-test

Scenarios we run

  • Ransomware
  • Business email compromise
  • Wire fraud
  • Cloud account takeover
  • Customer data breach
  • Insider threat
  • Vendor / supply chain breach
  • Lost or stolen device

Custom to your business

Scenarios built around your systems, data, vendors and the threats that actually target your industry.

Executive & technical tracks

Run them together or separately, depending on who needs to practice what.

Realistic escalation

New developments (“injects”) arrive as the exercise unfolds, just like a real incident.

Facilitated remotely

Run live over video with teams anywhere in the US.

Questions

Who should attend?

Usually leadership, IT, and whoever owns legal, finance, HR and communications. We'll recommend a list based on the scenario.

Do we need an incident response plan first?

No. A tabletop is a great way to find out what your plan needs to say. We can help you write or update it afterward.

Is it technical?

Executive sessions focus on decisions and communication, with no technical background needed. Technical sessions go deeper into investigation and containment.
