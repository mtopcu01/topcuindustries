Blue Team & Defense

Make your environment harder to break into and faster to recover. Hardening, monitoring and detection built by certified incident handlers and forensic analysts.

Most breaches at growing companies don't need advanced attackers. They take advantage of default settings, missing MFA, logs nobody reads and alerts nobody tunes.

Our team holds GIAC incident handling, forensics, intrusion analysis, cloud threat detection and continuous monitoring certifications. We bring that experience to your environment, working with the tools you already have.

Good fit if

  • No dedicated security team: IT generalists or an MSP wearing the security hat
  • You bought security tools but aren't sure they're configured or watched properly
  • Ransomware readiness is a concern from leadership or your insurer

What we do

Hardening review

Identity, endpoints, email, Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace and cloud configuration checked against proven baselines.

Logging & monitoring

Make sure the right logs exist, are kept long enough, and actually reach someone who can act on them.

Detection engineering

Custom detections for your environment, including cloud and identity threats, tuned to cut noise.

Incident response readiness

IR plans, playbooks and contact trees, so the first hour of an incident isn't spent figuring out who to call.

Security automation

SOAR and scripted playbooks that handle repetitive triage so your team can focus on real threats.

Purple teaming

We simulate attacker techniques and tune your defenses live, side by side with your team.

Questions

We don't have a security team. Is this for us?

Yes. That's who we built it for. We work directly with IT staff, MSPs and engineering teams and leave you with things they can maintain.

What tools do you work with?

We're tool-agnostic. We work with what you already have, such as Microsoft Defender, CrowdStrike, SentinelOne, Google Workspace, AWS or Azure, before recommending anything new.
