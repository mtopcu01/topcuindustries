Security Consulting & vCISO

Senior security guidance when you need it. Strategy, roadmaps, policies and someone to answer the hard questions, without hiring a full-time CISO.

Growing companies hit a point where security questions pile up: customer questionnaires, insurance forms, investor due diligence, “should we buy this tool?” But a full-time security executive isn't in the budget yet.

A virtual CISO (vCISO) gives you that expertise part-time. We learn your business, build a realistic plan, and stay on as your security lead for as long as you need.

Good fit if

  • Startups fielding security questionnaires from enterprise prospects
  • SMBs that need a security program but don't know where to start
  • Teams preparing for fundraising, acquisition or a first audit

How we help

Program assessment & roadmap

Where you stand today and a prioritized 90-day and 12-month plan, sized to your budget.

Policies & standards

Practical security policies people will actually follow, ready for audits.

Customer security reviews

Help answering security questionnaires and joining customer security calls.

Tool & vendor selection

Independent advice on what to buy and what to skip.

Leadership & board reporting

Clear updates on risk and progress for executives, boards and investors.

Cyber insurance support

Help with applications, renewals and meeting control requirements.

Questions

Is this one-time or ongoing?

Either. Some clients want a single assessment and roadmap. Others keep us on as their security lead month to month.

How is this different from an MSP?

An MSP runs your IT. We set security direction, help your MSP or IT team do the right things, and check that it's working.
