Questions

Type I or Type II?

Type I checks your controls at a point in time. Type II checks they worked over a period, usually 3 to 12 months. Many startups start with Type I to unblock deals, then move to Type II.

Do we need a compliance automation platform?

Not always. They help with evidence collection, but they don't write your policies or fix your controls. We work with or without one.
